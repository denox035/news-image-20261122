# news-image-20261122

One image summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026:
the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya, Iranian strikes on
Bahrain and tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, a Russian drone strike
near Kyiv, a deadly factory fire in Indonesia, BepiColombo reaching
Mercury, and Friday's market sell-off.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The poster itself was
drawn programmatically with PIL — every letter is rendered, nothing
is AI-generated text.

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261122`.
